Start with your home's needs

Tell us which appliances and household needs you want to support, the generator model if selected, and whether you already own the equipment. We assess electrical capacity, transfer equipment and any related service upgrades before defining your installation scope.

Electrical capacity

The generator and the loads you want to support guide the assessment of your home's electrical system.

Automatic transfer switch

Transfer equipment and its compatibility are part of the installation scope, rather than an assumption based on generator size alone.

Electrical work and project coordination

Your written proposal identifies the electrical work, equipment, required permit and inspection steps, and the responsibilities for gas work, startup and commissioning. Gas-system requirements and any utility upgrades must be evaluated by the appropriate qualified provider.

Standby systems and portable-generator connections

A permanently installed standby system and automatic transfer switch differ from a portable-generator inlet and interlock. We discuss the system you have or plan to buy and the appropriate installation path for your home.
