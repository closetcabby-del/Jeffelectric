What to expect from residential electrical troubleshooting

Why start with diagnosis?

A dead outlet, flickering light or tripping breaker can have more than one cause. Describe what happens, which rooms or devices are affected, and when the issue started. A diagnostic visit focuses on understanding the electrical concern before choosing a repair; a symptom by itself is not a confirmed diagnosis.

Is the repair included in diagnosis?

Diagnostic work and the resulting repair can be separate scopes. Before booking, ask what the diagnostic visit includes and how any recommended repair will be priced and approved. Jeff Electric explains the findings and next steps so you can decide how to proceed.

What should I do before contacting you?

Write down the symptoms and any recent change, such as new equipment or work in the home. Share photos only when they can be taken safely with equipment covers in place. If there is smoke, fire or an immediate danger, move away and contact emergency services. Do not remove electrical covers or repeatedly reset a breaker to investigate.
