When a panel upgrade may make sense

Adding electrical capacity

New equipment, EV charging or other electrical loads can create a need to evaluate the home’s available capacity.

Older or problematic equipment

Physical condition, recurring electrical issues or planned modernization can justify having the existing service equipment evaluated.

Subpanel needs

A properly planned subpanel can support additional circuits or organize electrical capacity for a specific area of the home.

Preparing for a panel assessment

Tell us what you want to add or what has changed in your home. Mention planned EV charging, new equipment, recurring problems, or an existing subpanel. This helps frame the assessment; a panel replacement is not automatically the answer to every electrical concern.

The final scope depends on the existing equipment and the work needed. Ask which parts of the panel, service, meter, or riser are included in the recommendation before deciding how to proceed.
