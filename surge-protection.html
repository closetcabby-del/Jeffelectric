Choosing whole-home surge protection

How is it different from a plug-in protector?

A whole-home surge protective device is installed at the electrical system, while a plug-in protector serves equipment connected at that point of use. Discuss the existing equipment and what you want to protect so the recommendation fits your home. Neither approach is a guarantee against every electrical event.

Can it be added during panel work?

Surge protection can be discussed as part of a panel replacement or as a separate project. Compatibility, the condition of the existing equipment and the installation arrangement affect the recommendation. A photo of the closed panel and any existing surge device helps begin that discussion.

What should I ask about the proposed device?

Ask which device is proposed, how its status can be checked, and what manufacturer warranty terms or exclusions apply. Protection equipment, installation workmanship and any manufacturer equipment coverage are distinct subjects; review the specific terms rather than assume all losses are covered.
